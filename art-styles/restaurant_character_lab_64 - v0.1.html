01 — Better acting

Separate eyebrows, eyes, mouth, mustache, shoulder, hand and leg poses. Idle breathing, stepping, blinking, waving, and seated motion are code.

02 — Cheap variation

Shared body geometry, 1px outlines, one hard shadow step, and modular headwear, hair, accessories and palettes. Hundreds of variants do not need hundreds of animations.

03 — 64px is a budget

A little more detail helps, but character readability still comes from larger heads, exaggerated gesture and strong costume silhouette—not texture or gradients.

Prototype only · Canvas 2D · offline · no external images, fonts or libraries. Character references are fictionalized comic designs for an alternate-history satire.
