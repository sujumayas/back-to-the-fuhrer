# Backlog

## Art polish (after the full demo is ready)

These came out of the art lab v0.2 review. The art is approved as is. Fix these once the demo plays end to end.

- Faces are simple. Expressions only change during the gesture animation. Add more mouth and brow states and react to events (shock when a portal opens, anger when waiting).
- Walk cycles have 4 frames. Six frames would read smoother, especially in the side view.
- Sitting has a breathing loop but no seated gesture (raising a hand, eating, drinking).
- Night lighting uses smooth canvas gradients. Consider dithered or banded light for a stricter pixel look.
- Keyboard feel and collisions in the room were checked with screenshots, not hands-on play. Tune speed, acceleration and blocker sizes.
